Lekhya (previously SpendSense) is a simple, offline expense tracking app built for people who want clarity over their spending without sacrificing privacy.

Lekhya · /ˈleː.kʰ.jɐ/ comes from the Sanskrit word Lēkhya (लेख्य) which means "that which is documented".

We spend money every day, yet often lack proper documentation and therefore, a clear understanding of where, when, and on what it’s spent. Most apps only offer a macro-level summary - totals, charts, and trends - but real savings start with understanding the details.

Lekhya helps you build that clarity.

🔍 Understand Your Spending - Clearly
With Lekhya, you can:
• Log expenses in just a few seconds
• Categorise spending the way _you_ want
• View detailed, micro-level insights into your expenses
• Understand exactly where and on what your money goes

No clutter. No unnecessary features. Just useful insights.

🔒 Privacy Comes First
Lekhya is built with privacy as a core principle:
• 100% offline — no internet required
• No cloud sync
• No data collection
• No ads
• No tracking
Your data stays on your device, always.

🚫 What Lekhya Is NOT
• Not an AI-powered app. Just plain, deterministic logic written by a boring human. No LLMs.
• No automated expense logging. That would require SMS access, heavy processing, and internet access - all of which compromise privacy and accuracy.  
Lekhya avoids over-engineered, unreliable automation in favour of simplicity and control.

⚖️ The Trade-Off
Complete privacy comes at the cost of convenience.
You’ll need to build the habit of manually logging expenses.  
But that small effort pays off with:
• Better awareness
• Cleaner data
• Zero privacy compromises

> Logging an expense takes just 5 seconds - no backlog, no guilt.

If you value privacy, clarity, and simplicity, Lekhya is for you :)
